# Edited demo — three relationship instances
Owner: DS (producer/editor); CS supplies stable UI footage, AI supplies voice loop, Cyber verifies disclosure visuals. Deliver a polished 90-second 16:9 MP4, captioned version, clean version, and local backup. Target 1080p, readable UI, consistent sound levels; verify actual submission constraints before export. This document is a production plan; no video exists yet.

## Three complete stories (synthetic people and recordings)
1. Partners: Maya is away for school; Leo is busy in another city. Maya privately mentions academic disappointment she does not want to explain. She approves only “short call / this week.” Leo's agent finds compatible approved availability and suggests offering a short call. Do not expose the disappointment or imply that it caused the nudge in recipient text.
2. Friends: Jordan and Sam moved to different cities. Jordan privately journals about feeling disconnected. Jordan approves “shared activity / this week.” Sam receives a suggestion to offer a shared activity; the friends choose their familiar online game themselves.
3. Family: A student away from home and a parent want to stay close without daily life reports. The parent privately journals about a tiring week and approves “encouraging note / no preference.” The student gets a simple prompt to offer a note. No health inference or hidden incident is exported.

| 42–55s | Labeled animation of actual bounded agent states, then live inbox | Explain coordination; never fabricate a live trace |
| 55–72s | Friends and family instances, each journal → approval → nudge | Prove one product serves three relationships |

| 83–90s | Human chooses to act; clean product close | “A little context. A better moment to connect.” |
