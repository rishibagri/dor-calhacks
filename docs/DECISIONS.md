- D041 (Cyber, Core 2; implemented offline, CS/AI consumer review requested; supersedes my unpublished Core 1 pause proposal): durable person-specific recipient pause per `docs/features/RECIPIENT_PAUSE.md` "Must hold" 1–6.
  - **Stored:** private `recipient_pause` with `{pause_id, owner, pair_id, target, version, expires_at, resumed_at?, created_at, updated_at}`. One row per owner + pair; the target is derived server-side from the pair. There is no reason, note, journal or model field.
  - **Duration:** 15 min to 30 days, always with an owner-visible end time.
  - **Owner-only reducers:** `pause_recipient({pair_id, expires_at, expected_version})` and `resume_recipient({pair_id, expected_version})`. Owner-only view `my_recipient_pauses {pair_id, version, expires_at, active, created_at}`.
  - **Enforcement:**
    - `approve_signal` refuses first (`unauthorized: recipient_paused`, owner-visible only);
    - `worker_signal_context` omits paused pairs' signals, so the worker needs no change;
    - `commit_nudge` rechecks in its own transaction and refuses with the same `stale: revoked` as a revoked approval;
    - `my_nudges` hides nudges whose source sender is pausing.
  - **No resurrection:** pausing revokes the owner's standing approvals in the pair through `suppressPairWork`, with sender-only `suppressed` receipts, suppresses their nudges, and revokes the owner's C7 activity approvals in the pair.   - **Fail closed:** a row counts as in force unless explicitly resumed or known lapsed (reducer clock, or the sweep marker in clockless views). Expiry goes through `sweep_expired`.
  - **Lookups:** `recipient_pause.owner` index then pair, with no new whole-table scans on hot paths (the worker view and sweep may scan).
